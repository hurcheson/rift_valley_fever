# Rift Valley fever — undergraduate thesis archive

This repository contains materials from my undergraduate mathematics research on **Rift Valley fever transmission dynamics**.

## Research context

The work examines disease transmission using mathematical models, including host–vector interactions and intervention scenarios. The broader research involves compartmental modelling, reproduction-number analysis, equilibrium and stability considerations, sensitivity analysis, and numerical simulations.

## Status and scope

- **Origin:** Undergraduate B.Sc. Mathematics research, University of Mines and Technology, Ghana.
- **Related manuscript:** Accepted for publication, **forthcoming** as of November 2026. The final title, author list, journal citation and publication link will be added once confirmed.
